Chick-fil-A breakfast hours

At most restaurants, breakfast starts when the doors open, usually 6:30 a.m., and ends at 10:30 a.m., Monday to Saturday. Every Chick-fil-A is closed on Sunday, so Saturday is the last breakfast of the week. Restaurants in malls, airports, colleges and other venues often open later and may serve a shorter breakfast, or none at all. The live checker at the top of this page uses your device's clock and lets you enter your own restaurant's hours. For a state-by-state look at opening times, holiday changes and exceptions, read the Chick-fil-A Breakfast Hours guide.

Biscuit, Chick-n-Minis or Egg White Grill?

The three most popular breakfasts suit different mornings:

Chicken Biscuit$4.95 · 460 cal

A breaded chicken filet on a warm buttermilk biscuit. The cheapest classic and the one most people grab on the way to work.

Chick-n-Minis (4 ct)$5.65 · 360 cal

Bite-sized nugget pieces in small yeast rolls with honey-butter spread. Easy to share and easy to eat in the car.

Egg White Grill$6.45 · 300 cal

Grilled chicken, egg whites and American cheese on a toasted multigrain English muffin. The lightest entree on the breakfast menu.

If you are very hungry, the Hash Brown Scramble Burrito (about 700 calories) is the most filling item. It packs scrambled eggs, hash browns, a cheese blend and your choice of protein into a flour tortilla. The Bowl has the same filling without the tortilla. Every price and option is listed on the full Chick-fil-A breakfast prices page.

Lighter breakfast choices

  • Egg White Grill: about 300 calories.
  • Chick-n-Minis (4 ct): about 360 calories, and you can share them.
  • Fruit Cup instead of Hash Browns saves about 200 calories.
  • Black hot coffee has almost no calories. Cream, sugar and flavoured iced coffees add more.
  • Skip the extra sauce or jelly if you are counting calories.

Coffee and breakfast drinks

Hot coffee is about $2.25 and iced coffee about $3.45. Simply Orange Juice, 1% milk and chocolate milk are also on the morning menu. Coffee is the drink most people add to breakfast. Over six days it adds roughly $13–$21 a week, so the week planner lets you see that cost before you commit. Coffee sizes and drink options are covered on the Chick-fil-A Coffee Menu page.

Tips for ordering breakfast

  1. Order ahead in the app during the morning rush to skip the line.
  2. Don't cut it close: arrive a little before 10:30 so your order goes in while breakfast is still on.
  3. Customise: swap the filet for spicy, add or remove cheese, or ask for no butter on the biscuit.
  4. Earn rewards: Chick-fil-A One members collect points on breakfast orders too.

Breakfast for a group

For office mornings and early events, many restaurants offer breakfast items for groups, such as trays of Chick-n-Minis and biscuits. These normally need to be ordered in advance. For smaller groups, a few 4-count Chick-n-Minis and some Hash Browns go a long way. The week planner above also works as a quick budget check: set the side and drink, then multiply one day's cost by the number of people.

Chick-fil-A breakfast FAQ

What time does Chick-fil-A serve breakfast?

Most Chick-fil-A restaurants serve breakfast from opening, usually 6:30 a.m., until 10:30 a.m., Monday to Saturday. Some locations open later or have different hours.

Does Chick-fil-A serve breakfast on Sunday?

No. Chick-fil-A restaurants are closed every Sunday, so there is no breakfast that day.

How much is a Chick-fil-A Chicken Biscuit?

A Chicken Biscuit costs about $4.95 in the U.S. and has about 460 calories. Prices vary by restaurant.

What is the healthiest Chick-fil-A breakfast?

The Egg White Grill is the lightest breakfast entree at about 300 calories. Pair it with a Fruit Cup (about 70 calories) and black coffee for a lighter morning meal.

Can I order Chick-fil-A breakfast after 10:30?

Usually not. Most restaurants switch to the lunch menu at 10:30 a.m. Hash Browns and breakfast sandwiches are not normally available after that time.

What comes in Chick-n-Minis?

Chick-n-Minis are small, warm yeast rolls brushed with a honey-butter spread and filled with bite-sized pieces of Chick-fil-A Nuggets. The 4-count is the standard order and larger counts are available.

Is this the official Chick-fil-A website?

No. This is an independent, unofficial breakfast menu guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chick-fil-A, Inc.
